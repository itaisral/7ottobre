A source no one can call pro-Israel

HRW has also accused Israel
Human Rights Watch · ReportIsrael's Crime of Extermination, Acts of Genocide in GazaPublished on 19 December 2024

On 19 December 2024 Human Rights Watch published a report accusing the Israeli authorities of deliberately depriving the population of Gaza of adequate access to water since October 2023, destroying water and sanitation facilities and restricting the entry of fuel and electricity. According to HRW, this conduct most likely caused thousands of deaths and amounts to the crime against humanity of extermination and to acts of genocide. The Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs rejected the accusations, calling them lies.

We cite this report to make clear what kind of source we are quoting. Human Rights Watch is among the organizations most critical of Israel, and its conclusions on 7 October cannot be dismissed as Israeli propaganda. It is this same organization that calls Hamas's version false and documents the massacre of civilians.

Original source

Human Rights Watch, “Israel's Crime of Extermination, Acts of Genocide in Gaza”, 19 December 2024. The page is in English.
